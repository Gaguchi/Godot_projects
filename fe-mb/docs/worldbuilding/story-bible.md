## Pillars (locked)
- **Genre/feel:** grounded medieval fantasy, Mount & Blade energy. No cataclysm, no cosmic stakes. Dragons are the ONE fantastical element and they are extinct legend.
- **Tone:** earthy + gallows humor over a warm found-family core. Hard world, people who like each other. NOT the candle-lit grimdark the current UI leans into (UI direction to be revisited — the user isn't a fan of the candlelit look).
- **The wandering home:** a **free mercenary company** — the party is a travelling outfit that exists while it moves. Towns hire it (battles are jobs / ambushes); misfits join the roster.
- **Map:** **omnidirectional open sandbox** (M&B-style) — NOT a one-way west march. Travel any direction. (Note: current node-map + food-per-hop tech assumes directed travel; this is a design tension to resolve.)
- **The MC:** authored **foundling**, **player-named**. War-orphan raised in the company's wagons; the company is the only home they've known, the Captain the only parent. Player names them at the guild-registration beat.
- **Companions:** BG3-style — collected through **main-quest and big side/faction quests**, each a written individual with their own recruitment story. Not shop-row hires.
- **Player names the (new) company** later, at a guild, once they've gathered a few companions.

## The spine — the hunt (locked)
The MC's father-figure Captain is killed by his **sworn brother** (the company's second-in-command) over the company's greatest score. The MC inherits the banner and hunts the killer across the open map (main questline), while working as a free company (sandbox).

### The inciting job (locked)
1. The company takes its biggest-ever job: recover **loot stolen from a minor monarch**. Payout larger than every previous job combined.
2. Brutal fight — they **best a rival mercenary company** for the loot. During it, the **future antagonist (the sworn brother) saves the MC's life** on the field. (The MC owes him. This aches later.)
3. That night the MC overhears a **shouting match** between the Captain and his oldest friend (the 2iC). It escalates; the **Captain is stabbed**; the 2iC leaves with **the loot chest** (gold & jewels). The camp erupts — **civil war within the company, brother against brother** (the split is NOT clean; the 2iC is beloved and competent, and his story is plausible, so good people side with him).
4. The MC, hidden, reaches the dying Captain. The Captain refuses aid, presses a large **egg-shaped stone** into his hands: guard this, no matter what — the gold the traitors took is worth *nothing* beside it. Flee.
5. The MC doesn't know it yet: it's a **dragon egg**.

### The egg (locked)
- Dragons: **extinct ~300 years**; bones over castle gates, names on old banners, histories most treat as myth. A living dragon = whoever holds it holds tomorrow.
- The egg was the monarch's true secret — the reason the job was worth a fortune (not the gold). Nature possibly unknown even to the client. (proposed wiring — confirm.)

### LOCKED cast + world (session 2)
- **Captain:** **Corwin Vale**, founder of **the Ashford Company** on a code (take the job, keep the word, refuse the jobs you don't take).
- **Traitor / 2iC / final boss:** **Mercer Kaine**, sworn brother. The killing was **deliberate** — Vale wouldn't yield the egg, and Kaine chose. No self-deception; he'd do it again. He saved the foundling on the loot-field the same afternoon he murdered Vale — his conviction runs *above* affection, which is why he can't be talked down.
- **Re-ally companion:** **Bex Callan**, the veteran duelist who taught the foundling the blade (lead name).
- **Sub-boss (turnable/tragic):** **Cassius Merrow**, the old company's champion, a dragon-scar true believer (lead name).
- **The egg stays a SECRET** — no public dragon-rush. The world is ordinary petty politics; the egg-hunt runs underneath as a quiet war of spies and survivors.
- **Political map:** a handful of **petty realms at low-grade border war** (classic M&B) — endless merc work, no side purely right.
- **The client:** a **petty king who KNOWS exactly what the egg is** — his house guarded the "stone" for generations, kept it from every greater crown. Wants it back desperately and silently; becomes a player once he learns the MC has it.
- **Kaine's power = an escalation ladder (all three):** Act I the Ashford name + the loyal half of the old roster (peer hunting you); Act II a **patron lord** (reach, open doors); Act III an **old anti-dragon order** (zealots + creed + resources; Cassius's home).

### The antagonist (locked direction)
- **Not greed.** The sworn brother understands the danger dragons pose. He wants the egg to **make sure it never hatches — to destroy it before it can grow**. He has read the old accounts no one believes. He is *not wrong* that a dragon is catastrophe — that's the horror of him.
- He will **not return the egg to the client** (a crown would raise a weapon that ends kingdoms); he means to **end it**.
- He **holds the old company's name** — legally he is the old outfit now, trading on 30 years of its reputation (the guild-registration beat forces the MC to admit the name is his).
- He runs a **shadow-war of information** — spies, bounties on old-company survivors, questions in taverns — **hunting confirmation** of what the Captain really hid, and **hunting the MC's company** in-story and in-game.
- Fought as the **final boss** (existing boss slot). Lieutenants → regional sub-bosses.

## Companions (roster frame — ~7, standard; each joins via a main/faction quest, BG3-style)
Tactical roles map to existing classes. Two familiar faces from the old company (Oak + the runaway); the rest tie into the world's factions.

### Bagrat "Oak" — the shield / protector *(tank)* — LOCKED as first companion
- **Role in story:** an old-company (Ashford) survivor; **the first companion the MC reunites with** (took the slot originally sketched for Bex — Bex is deprioritized for now, not cut).
- **Look:** a barrel-chested old man, tough as nails; carries a **massive oak shield** into battle — that's why everyone just calls him **Oak** (Bagrat is his given name; "Oak" is the earned byname). Georgian-rooted first name to match his ability.
- **Personality:** deeply kind and lovable, gentle; a protector who **does not enjoy violence** but wants to shield people. Calls the ones he loves *genatsvale* (Georgian term of endearment ≈ "I'd take your place").
- **Signature ability — Genatsvale:** each turn, the first blow aimed at an **adjacent ally** lands on **him** instead (per ally, per turn) — he shields allies with his body. The word he calls people, made literal. Rhymes with the MC's **Not Today**: Oak already does with his body what the foundling is learning to do.
- **Open:** his past (gentle-always vs. once-feared-and-atoning), his role on the betrayal night, and exactly where/how the MC finds him.
